# References

Only works checked by a separate pass, recorded in `docs/_citation_verdicts.md` (a working file, not part of the argument). Each entry is one the pass marked verified. The pass's URLs are where it looked; they are not a claim that every public copy is stable.

- Tobias Adrian and Hyun Song Shin. "Liquidity and Leverage." *Journal of Financial Intermediation*, 2010. Also FRBNY Staff Report 328.
- Robert U. Ayres and Benjamin Warr. "Accounting for Growth: The Role of Physical Work." *Structural Change and Economic Dynamics*, 2005.
- Ben Bernanke, Mark Gertler, and Simon Gilchrist. "The Financial Accelerator and the Flight to Quality." *Review of Economics and Statistics*, 1996. NBER Working Paper 4789, 1994.
- Markus K. Brunnermeier and Lasse Heje Pedersen. "Market Liquidity and Funding Liquidity." *Review of Financial Studies*, 2009.
- Thomas M. Cover and Joy A. Thomas. *Elements of Information Theory*, chapter 6, "Gambling and Data Compression." 2nd ed., 2006.
- Tri Vi Dang, Gary Gorton, and Bengt Holmström. "Ignorance, Debt, and Financial Crises." Working paper; related survey in the *Annual Review of Financial Economics*, 2020.
- Henry Ford. Interview, *New-York Tribune*, 4 December 1921. Remarks on an energy currency in place of gold.
- John Fullarton. *On the Regulation of Currencies*, 1844.
- Nicholas Georgescu-Roegen. *The Entropy Law and the Economic Process*. Harvard University Press, 1971.
- Simon Gilchrist and Egon Zakrajšek. "Credit Spreads and Business Cycle Fluctuations." *American Economic Review*, 2012.
- Charles A. E. Goodhart. "Problems of Monetary Management: The U.K. Experience." In *Papers in Monetary Economics*, Reserve Bank of Australia, 1975. Restated in *Monetary Theory and Practice*, 1984. The common slogan is Marilyn Strathern's later paraphrase, not Goodhart's wording.
- Gary Gorton. *Slapped by the Invisible Hand: The Panic of 2007*. Oxford University Press, 2010.
- David Graeber. *Debt: The First 5,000 Years*. Melville House, 2011.
- Robin Hanson. "Logarithmic Market Scoring Rules for Modular Combinatorial Information Aggregation." *Journal of Prediction Markets*, 2007.
- Robin Hanson and Ryan Oprea. "A Manipulator Can Aid Prediction Market Accuracy." *Economica*, 2009.
- F. A. Hayek. *Denationalisation of Money*. Institute of Economic Affairs, 1976; refined edition 1978.
- Bengt Holmström. "Understanding the Role of Debt in the Financial System." BIS Working Paper 479, 2015.
- J. L. Kelly Jr. "A New Interpretation of Information Rate." *Bell System Technical Journal*, 1956.
- John Maynard Keynes. International Clearing Union proposals, including the bancor, 1941–43. Not a commodity-basket standard.
- Nobuhiro Kiyotaki and John Moore. "Credit Cycles." *Journal of Political Economy*, 1997.
- Nobuhiro Kiyotaki and Randall Wright. "On Money as a Medium of Exchange." *Journal of Political Economy*, 1989.
- Narayana R. Kocherlakota. "Money Is Memory." *Journal of Economic Theory*, 1998.
- Ricardo Lagos and Randall Wright. "A Unified Framework for Monetary Theory and Policy Analysis." *Journal of Political Economy*, 2005.
- Rolf Landauer. "Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process." *IBM Journal of Research and Development*, 1961.
- Francis A. Longstaff, Sanjay Mithal, and Eric Neis. "Corporate Yield Spreads: Default Risk or Liquidity?" *Journal of Finance*, 2005. Default is the majority of the spread in their categories, not a small residual.
- Robert E. Lucas Jr. "Econometric Policy Evaluation: A Critique." *Carnegie-Rochester Conference Series on Public Policy*, 1976.
- Lloyd W. Mints. *A History of Banking Theory in Great Britain and the United States*. University of Chicago Press, 1945.
- Hyman P. Minsky. "The Financial Instability Hypothesis." Levy Economics Institute Working Paper 74. Developed in *Stabilizing an Unstable Economy*, 1986.
- Marco Ottaviani and Peter Norman Sørensen. "Aggregation of Information and Beliefs: Asset Pricing Lessons from Prediction Markets." University of Copenhagen discussion paper. Related work in *AEJ: Micro*, 2010.
- Thomas J. Sargent and Neil Wallace. "The Real-Bills Doctrine versus the Quantity Theory: A Reconsideration." *Journal of Political Economy*, 1982.
- Henry Thornton. *An Enquiry into the Nature and Effects of the Paper Credit of Great Britain*, 1802.
- Justin Wolfers and Eric Zitzewitz. "Interpreting Prediction Market Prices as Probabilities." NBER Working Paper 12200, 2006.
